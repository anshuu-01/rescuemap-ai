What is RescueMap AI & What Purpose Does It Serve?

RescueMap AI is a real-time, AI-powered disaster management and mitigation web application designed for emergency response teams (e.g., NDRF, SDRF, District Collectors, and First Responders).

During large-scale disasters like cyclones or floods, commanders face an overwhelming volume of conflicting disaster reports with limited rescue assets. RescueMap AI addresses this critical bottleneck:

  • 🛸 Drone & Satellite Imagery Ingestion: Accepts aerial reconnaissance photos uploaded by field teams or autonomous drones.
  • 🤖 Multimodal AI Computer Vision: Leverages Google Gemini 2.0 Flash to instantly detect structural collapse, flood levels, submerged roads, and downed power infrastructure.
  • 🎯 Algorithmic Priority Scoring (0–100): Ranks affected sites from Tier S (Immediate Emergency) to Tier C (Monitor) using a multi-factor decision matrix.
  • 🗺️ Live Geospatial Situation Room: Maps all active sites on an interactive Leaflet map with spatial popup analytics and satellite overlays.
💡 Core Value Proposition: Converts raw drone photos into prioritized, actionable rescue routes in under 60 seconds — eliminating manual reporting friction and saving critical lives during the golden hours of disaster relief.

Core Application Features

🗺️
Geospatial Situation Map
Interactive OpenStreetMap satellite layer powered by Leaflet.js with dynamic tier-coded SVG pin markers (Tier S Red to Tier C Green).
🤖
Gemini Vision AI Engine
Direct client-side integration with Google Gemini 2.0 Flash for zero-latency structural damage classification and reasoning generation.
📊
Multi-Factor Priority Scoring
Dynamic scoring matrix combining structural damage, road accessibility, hospital proximity, population density, and vulnerable demographic count.
📂
Field Upload with EXIF Extraction
Upload portal with ExifReader.js to extract GPS lat/long metadata automatically from camera photos.
🖼️
Before / After Slider Comparison
Interactive split-screen visual comparison tool to contrast pre-disaster satellite images against post-disaster drone reconnaissance photos.
📣
AI Voice Situation Briefing
Native Web Speech API text-to-speech engine providing audible voice briefings of current disaster assessments for hands-free operations.
📄
CSV & Audit Trail Export
Export ranked priority assessments as standardized CSV data for regional command centers, accompanied by transparent audit logs.
📱
Mobile QR Tunnel Sharing
Built-in QR canvas generator using QRious to allow judges and commanders to launch the responsive mobile web app on personal phones instantly.

The AI Priority Scoring Matrix

Sites are evaluated programmatically on a 0–100 scale based on weighted critical factors:

Evaluation Factor Weight Scoring Logic
Structural Damage Severity35%Destroyed (100) > Major (75) > Moderate (50) > Minor (25)
Road Blockage / Accessibility20%Severely Blocked (100) > Partially Blocked (60) > Clear (10)
Population Density15%High density urban zones score higher urgency
Vulnerable Group Count15%Normalized count of elderly, pediatric, and hospital patients
Hospital Distance Offset10%Increases score as distance to functioning medical facility grows
Route Isolation Factor5%Absence of alternate access roads adds isolation weight
Tier Score Range Operational Classification
Tier S 80 – 100 Immediate Emergency — Direct Life Threat & Complete Isolation
Tier A 60 – 79 High Priority — Severe Structural Damage or Major Obstruction
Tier B 40 – 59 Moderate Urgency — Managed Access & Partial Service Disruption
Tier C 0 – 39 Low Threat — Operational Facilities & Minor Surface Damage

How RescueMap AI Was Built From Scratch

  • 1

    Conceptualization & Problem Definition

    Identified the core disaster bottleneck: delay in converting raw field imagery into prioritized rescue dispatches. Designed a streamlined single-page situation room workflow.

  • 2

    UI Framework & Dark Mode Foundation

    Configured Tailwind CSS in a single index.html template alongside Google Fonts (Inter, Outfit, JetBrains Mono) for a modern military-grade tactical aesthetics.

  • 3

    React Context & State Store Setup

    Created DisasterContext to manage global state seamlessly across map elements, site cards, modal dialogs, and AI configuration flags.

  • 4

    Leaflet Map & Custom Marker Pipeline

    Integrated Leaflet.js with OpenStreetMap layers. Built dynamic SVG map markers color-coded to reflect site risk tiers in real time.

  • 5

    Gemini Vision API Integration

    Wrote the analyzeImageWithGemini() function to stream base64 images to Google's Gemini Flash 2.0 API, receiving structured JSON payloads specifying damage and road blockages.

  • 6

    Priority Scoring & Classification Engine

    Developed the computePriorityScore() mathematical model combining AI damage output with spatial and demographic vulnerability parameters.

  • 7

    EXIF GPS Metadata Extraction

    Implemented ExifReader.js inside the image uploader to automatically extract camera location data, instantly pinning photos on the map upon upload.

  • 8

    Before/After Visual Comparison & Interactive Features

    Created interactive split-range sliders for visual destruction verification, voice audio briefing components using Web Speech API, and CSV reporting capabilities.
